ELOTE CHICKEN PASTA SALAD

PREP TIME: 15 MINUTES

TOTAL TIME: 30 MINUTES

COOK TIME: 15 MINUTES

YIELDS: 6

This elote chicken pasta salad is loaded with roasted corn, jalapeño, red onion, crumbled cotija, and juicy seasoned chicken, all tossed in a smoky chipotle-sour cream dressing. It's inspired by Mexican street corn — creamy, tangy, a little spicy, and packed with flavor in every single bite. The perfect cold pasta salad to bring to a summer cookout or meal prep for the week.
